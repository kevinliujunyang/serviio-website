What you are authorizing

Serviio requests access to your business profile and locations, menu items and prices, orders, and payments. This supports order submission, Square-hosted checkout, and checking payment status. Square handles your sign-in and approval.

The permissions allow creating orders and payments. Customers enter payment details on Square’s hosted checkout page. You can disconnect below.

Connecting alone does not place orders or charge payments. Voice ordering and kitchen workflows require separate setup.
