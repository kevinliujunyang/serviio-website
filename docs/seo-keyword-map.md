# Serviio SEO Keyword Map

## Strategy

Serviio should target every meaningful keyword combination across restaurant type, AI phone use case, POS integration, language, and location. Do not create a separate thin landing page for every phrase. Use a matrix approach:

- Build one strong page for each distinct search intent.
- Cover close variants in page titles, H1/H2 copy, FAQs, schema, internal links, and anchor text.
- Create separate pages only when the searcher intent is meaningfully different, such as Chinese restaurant owners, POS integration, specific POS systems, no-POS leads, or service areas.

The highest-value buyer is still a Chinese restaurant owner in the United States who already uses a POS system and receives meaningful phone-order volume. Broader terms like "restaurant tech" and "restaurant AI assistant" should be targeted, but they are higher competition and should support the conversion pages instead of replacing the niche wedge.

## Keyword Combination Formula

Use this formula to generate target phrases:

`[restaurant type] + [AI/voice category] + [phone/order use case] + [POS/integration qualifier] + [language/location qualifier]`

Examples:

- Chinese restaurant AI phone ordering
- AI phone answering for Chinese restaurants
- restaurant AI phone order taker
- POS integrated AI phone agent restaurant
- restaurant POS system phone orders
- restaurant AI assistant for takeout orders
- Chinese restaurant POS integration AI phone ordering
- restaurant tech AI phone ordering for POS systems
- bilingual restaurant phone order AI
- AI voice assistant for restaurants using Square POS
