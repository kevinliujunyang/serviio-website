## Lead Qualification

All lead forms should preserve:
- restaurant name
- owner or manager name
- phone
- email
- city
- state
- current POS or POS status
- weekly phone order volume
- POS recommendation interest for restaurants without a POS
- lead source
- ideal customer profile

Priority lead definition:
- Chinese restaurant in the United States
- already uses 39 Miles, Square, Toast, Clover, MenuSifu, Chowbus, Mealkeyway, or another POS
- meaningful weekly phone order volume
- wants fewer missed calls or less manual entry

Secondary lead definition:
- no POS yet
- wants POS recommendations through `pos_recommendation_interest`
- can be nurtured or referred to a POS partner later

Operational lead scoring and follow-up steps live in `docs/seo-deploy-and-lead-runbook.md`.
