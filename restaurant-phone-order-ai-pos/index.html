A demonstration and acceptance checklist

  • Order two items with different quantities and choices.
  • Change one option and confirm the readback.
  • Check customer details and pickup arrangements.
  • Verify the receiving location, staff-visible order, and kitchen handoff.
  • Test unresolved requests, failed results, and staff transfer.

Arrange any real order or payment test explicitly with the restaurant. Reading a menu or seeing an authorization succeed is not end-to-end acceptance.

Questions before you get started

Which POS can I connect myself?

The Serviio account supports connecting Square, choosing a location, checking API access, and loading its menu. Availability for other POS systems must be confirmed for the restaurant and provider.

Does reading a menu verify kitchen delivery?

No. A menu read checks catalog access. It does not prove that order creation, payment, POS display, or kitchen printing works. Those steps need separate testing for your location.

What should we test about modifiers?

Use your menu’s required choices, sizes, included options, add-ons, substitutions, and quantity rules. Confirm the selected item and options, the resulting price, and what staff see on the received order.

What permissions does Square connection request?

The account explains access to your business profile and locations, menu items and prices, orders, and payments. These permissions allow order and payment creation. Customers enter payment details on Square-hosted checkout. Connecting alone does not place an order or charge a payment.

What if we use another POS or have no POS yet?

Share the current system and how orders reach the kitchen so we can check compatibility. If you have no POS, describe your existing process and ask for setup guidance before assuming an integration is available.
