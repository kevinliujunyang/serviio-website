Questions before you get started

Is Serviio a human answering service?

Serviio uses an AI voice agent for restaurant calls. It does not replace every staff conversation. Decide which requests need a person and test the transfer or message-taking workflow before launch.

What happens when a caller needs a person?

Configure when to offer a transfer to restaurant staff and what to do if the call is not answered. Where message-taking is configured, collect callback details and agree on who receives and follows up on the message.

Can it answer allergy or ingredient questions?

Provide restaurant-approved menu and ingredient information during setup. Uncertain allergy, ingredient, or substitution questions should go to staff. Do not treat an AI answer as a guarantee that a meal is allergen-free.

Can it handle more than one caller?

Serviio supports multiple simultaneous calls. Review the expected call volume, phone routing, and staff-transfer behavior for your restaurant and test those paths before launch.

Are calls without orders charged?

Under Serviio’s phone-ordering pricing, calls that do not result in a submitted order have no Serviio order fee. After the free trial, the fee is 2% per submitted order, with no monthly or setup fee. Separate phone or POS provider charges may apply.
