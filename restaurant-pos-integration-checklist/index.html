The readiness checklist

Answer these before choosing an AI phone agent or POS integration path. This is also a Chinese restaurant POS integration checklist and an AI phone ordering POS readiness checklist for owners comparing restaurant technology options.

1. POS system

Name the current POS and whether it supports online ordering, kitchen tickets, order notes, modifiers, and reporting.

2. Phone-order volume

Estimate weekly phone orders, missed calls, rush-hour spikes, and after-hours demand.

3. Order handoff

Decide whether confirmed orders should route to POS, kitchen workflow, SMS confirmation, or staff review first.

4. Menu complexity

List modifiers such as spice level, substitutions, combos, lunch specials, pickup time, and customer notes.

5. Language fit

Check whether callers need English, Mandarin, Cantonese, or bilingual restaurant phone ordering support.

6. No-POS routing

If there is no POS yet, route the owner to POS recommendations before treating the lead as AI-integration ready.
