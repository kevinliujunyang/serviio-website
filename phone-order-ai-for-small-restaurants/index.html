A small-restaurant launch checklist

  1. Prepare current menu items, prices, sold-out handling, and required choices.
  2. Record busy periods and current phone-order volume as a baseline.
  3. Confirm the POS or current kitchen handoff process.
  4. Test a changed order, an information call, and a staff transfer.
  5. Confirm staff know how to receive orders, handle exceptions, and update the menu.

Questions before you get started

Do I need a dedicated person to watch the AI?

Decide who receives orders, handles transfers and responds to unresolved requests. AI phone coverage can handle routine conversations, but staff responsibilities and fallback procedures still need to be defined.

When does the 2% fee apply?

After a free trial of at least three months, the Serviio fee is 2% per submitted phone order. There is no monthly or setup fee. Calls without a submitted order have no Serviio order fee. Separate phone or POS provider charges may apply.

Does the fee automatically save money on staffing?

No staffing saving is guaranteed. Compare the order fee with your actual call volume, time spent on calls, and how staff use that time. Use a trial to check the effect on your own operation.

Can I start if I already use Square?

You can create a Serviio account, save your restaurant details, connect Square and choose a location to load its menu. Phone ordering and kitchen handoff still need separate setup and testing.

What if my restaurant has no POS?

Describe how you currently record and deliver orders to the kitchen. Discuss the supported workflow and any POS recommendations with Serviio before deciding whether to proceed.
