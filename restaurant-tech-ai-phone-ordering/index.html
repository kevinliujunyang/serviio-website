From a phone call to a confirmed order

Answer the call

Greet the guest using your restaurant setup and establish whether they want to order, ask a menu question, or speak to staff.

Check menu choices

Resolve quantities, sizes, spice levels, sides, and required options. Recheck the details when the guest changes an item.

Confirm before submission

Read back the items, options, pickup arrangements, and contact details. Ask the guest to confirm, then use the configured submission and handoff workflow.

Example: changing an order on the call

Illustrative walkthrough of quantities and modifiers, not a recorded call or a live order. Items, options, and prices depend on your configured menu.

  1. Caller

    One Kung Pao Chicken, medium spice, with fried rice.

  2. Assistant

    One Kung Pao Chicken, medium, with fried rice. Anything else?

  3. Caller

    Make that two. One mild and one medium, both with fried rice.

  4. Assistant

    To confirm: two Kung Pao Chicken, one mild and one medium, both with fried rice. Let’s confirm your name, phone number, and pickup arrangements before submitting the order.

Questions before you get started

Does Serviio take orders or just answer questions?

Serviio can capture menu items, quantities, available options, pickup details, and customer contact information, then ask the caller to confirm. Order submission and kitchen delivery depend on the configured restaurant workflow.

How does it handle modifiers or unclear requests?

Menu setup defines available sizes, sides, spice levels, add-ons, and required choices. Demonstrate substitutions and order changes using your actual menu. When a request cannot be resolved, agree on a staff transfer or message-taking path before launch.

Can I keep my restaurant number?

Discuss forwarding and coverage for your existing number during setup. Test the incoming call, staff transfer, and fallback paths before switching restaurant traffic.

How much does phone ordering cost?

AI phone ordering has a free trial of at least three months. After the trial, Serviio charges 2% per submitted order, with no monthly or setup fee. Calls that do not result in a submitted order have no Serviio order fee. Confirm any separate phone or POS provider charges.

Does a Square connection turn on phone ordering?

No. Your account lets you connect Square, choose a location, and load its menu. Voice ordering, item options, and kitchen handoff require separate configuration and testing.
