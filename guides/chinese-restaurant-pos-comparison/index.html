A practical POS demo scorecard

For each provider, record demonstrated, needs setup or an add-on, or not demonstrated for each check below. Keep the quote and sample kitchen ticket beside your notes.

Bilingual menu and kitchen ticket

Enter a dish with English and Chinese names. Print or display its kitchen ticket and ask kitchen staff whether the item, quantity, and preparation notes are clear.

Combos, modifiers, and prices

Enter two lunch combos with different proteins, sides, and spice levels. Change one choice. Verify required options, additional charges, and the final total.

Phone-order details and changes

Attach a caller name, callback number, and pickup time. Change a quantity before confirming the order, and check how staff distinguish a correction from a second order.

Kitchen routing and connection problems

Ask the provider to show where the order appears and how staff know it arrived. Ask what happens if a printer or connection fails and how staff should recover without duplicating the ticket.

Full cost and data access

Request written costs for hardware, software, payment processing, support, integrations, and cancellation. Ask how you can export your menu and order data.

AI phone-order integration

Ask both the POS provider and the phone-order provider to confirm the supported connection for your exact setup. Test modifiers and kitchen delivery together before launch. See the restaurant POS integration checklist.

Chinese restaurant POS comparison questions

How should I compare Toast, MenuSifu, or Chowbus for a Chinese restaurant?

Give each provider the same menu and sample takeout order. Compare modifier entry, bilingual kitchen tickets, order changes, pickup details, and the full written quote. Choose based on demonstrated support for your workflow rather than a general brand ranking.

Should I replace my POS to add AI phone ordering?

First check whether your existing setup can support the order handoff you need. Ask about integration access, menu mapping, kitchen routing, and additional charges before considering a POS replacement.
