Chinese restaurant POS comparison for phone orders

Compare POS systems by how well they handle Chinese takeout phone orders, modifiers, kitchen handoff, and future AI phone ordering. This guide is for owners comparing 39 Miles, MenuSifu, Chowbus, Mealkeyway, Square, Toast, Clover, and related restaurant POS platforms.

What to compare before choosing a POS

Phone-order speed

How fast staff can enter caller name, phone number, pickup time, modifiers, and payment notes.

Chinese menu complexity

Support for combos, lunch specials, substitutions, spice level, sauces, family meals, and bilingual item names.

AI readiness

Whether menu data, order details, kitchen routing, and customer confirmations can support AI phone ordering later.

How to think about each POS type

Chinese-restaurant POS systems such as 39 Miles, MenuSifu, Chowbus, and Mealkeyway may fit restaurants with bilingual menus, Chinese takeout workflows, and cuisine-specific ordering patterns.

General restaurant POS systems such as Square, Toast, and Clover may fit owners who want broader ecosystem support, payments, hardware, and integrations.

No-POS restaurants should choose a system that can handle phone orders today and keep the door open for AI phone ordering once the menu and workflow are organized.
