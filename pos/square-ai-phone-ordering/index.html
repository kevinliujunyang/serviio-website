Square connection questions

Does connecting Square activate phone ordering?

Connecting Square alone does not place orders, charge payments, or activate phone ordering. Voice ordering and kitchen workflows require separate setup and testing.

What Square access does Serviio request?

Authorization covers your business profile and locations, menu items and prices, orders, and payments. These permissions allow creating orders and payments. Customers enter payment details on Square-hosted checkout. You can disconnect from your Serviio account.

How do I review my Square menu?

After signing in, save your restaurant details, connect Square, select a location, and choose Load menu. Results depend on that location’s catalog. Review items, prices, and options. Reading the menu does not create an order or payment.
