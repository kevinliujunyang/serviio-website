What to check from phone call to kitchen

1. Prepare the menu

Bring item names in the languages your customers use, prices, sizes, required choices, and sold-out items. Make spice levels and substitutions explicit so a caller’s request can be checked against your menu.

2. Try a realistic order

During a demonstration, ask for two dishes with different quantities, change a modifier, and ask the agent to repeat the order. Check the items, quantities, pickup details, and customer contact information together.

3. Verify the handoff

Agree on how confirmed orders reach staff or the kitchen. For a POS connection, check item mapping, required options, and ticket routing. Use the POS integration checklist to prepare.

Chinese restaurant answering service questions

Is this a human Chinese answering service?

Serviio uses an AI voice agent, not a human call center. It is designed for restaurant phone orders and menu questions. If you need general business reception or human interpretation, discuss those requirements before choosing a service.

How much does Serviio cost?

Serviio charges 2% per completed order, with no monthly fees or setup costs. For example, the Serviio fee on a $40 completed order is $0.80. Confirm any separate phone or POS provider charges with those providers.

Can customers order in English and Chinese?

Serviio supports English and Chinese conversations. Tell us whether your callers need Mandarin, Cantonese, or both, and include those languages in a demonstration using your restaurant menu.

Does it work with my existing POS?

Share your POS provider and current setup so we can check compatibility and order delivery. Naming a POS in our form is not confirmation of an available integration. Confirm the menu mapping and kitchen handoff before launch.
