Assistant-ordering questions

Can guests place live restaurant orders through AI assistants today?

The experience is currently available as a demo using test orders. Demo orders are not sent to a restaurant and do not create customer payments. Live ordering depends on restaurant onboarding, platform availability, and end-to-end testing.

Does connecting Square automatically list my restaurant in ChatGPT or Claude?

No. Connecting Square prepares account and menu access. It does not establish platform availability, an AI recommendation, or active phone ordering. Platform access and restaurant availability must be confirmed separately.

How is assistant ordering different from AI phone ordering?

Phone ordering handles calls to your restaurant’s phone number. Assistant ordering lets guests browse a menu and review an order through an assistant conversation. The two products have separate onboarding and activation requirements.

Does the 2% phone-ordering price apply to assistant ordering?

The published 2% fee and three-month free trial apply to AI phone ordering. Contact us to discuss availability and pricing for assistant ordering.
